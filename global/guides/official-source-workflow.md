# Updating durable guidance

Load only when changing durable configuration or guidance. Find the narrowest current official source; record its URL, version, retrieval status and relevant claim in the registry. Separate documented behavior from local policy and historical observations. Read changed relevant content, not the entire source archive. Prepare a reviewable candidate and validation evidence before applying an approved update. Access failure is not proof of no change.

Primary design source: [Astra skills and prompts](https://developers.openai.com/blog/rethinking-skills-and-prompts-for-gpt-6-astra). Configuration authority: [Codex reference](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/config-file/config-reference). Third-party repositories describe their own tools, not OpenAI-endorsed performance.
